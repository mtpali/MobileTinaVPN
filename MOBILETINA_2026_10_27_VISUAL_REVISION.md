# UI follow-up — October 27, 2026

This follow-up supersedes earlier records for the navigation-logo gesture and notification styling. Version stays 2.3.13 / 753. Both editions use a compact white stop button (140 × 32dp visual, 48dp tap target) with black bold text in collapsed and expanded notification content. Server overflow labels are Persian. The server share dialog offers only QR and copy; the Android share-sheet option is removed. The navigation-logo hold, its reveal/hide timers and its hidden subscription menu entry are removed. The independent two-second subscription-header QR gesture and protected-source rules remain.

Only MobileTina replaces its icon with the newly supplied 1254px image, packaged losslessly. Two additional zoom-out steps increase the adaptive inset from 14% to 18% (two 2-percentage-point steps per edge); its original canvas and proportions stay intact. AliMTP images are unchanged. MobileTina's custom About page uses white light-mode surfaces with dark text and separate night resources; its brand/link text is unchanged.

Source/XML/artwork checks and the existing full migration workflow validate the changes. No on-device acceptance or permanent signing key is claimed. Both PRs remain draft and unmerged.
